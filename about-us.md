**About Us**  
  
Based in London, Ontario, we provide live String music for weddings, corporate functions, parties and other special occasions. Our musicians perform with London Symphonia and other professional ensembles.  
  
**What Sets Us Apart** - Wedding music selections should be as personalised as the wedding itself, and so we help you select pieces to reflect your tastes.   
  
Aside from our extensive performance experience, our large, varied library consists of classical standards and an always growing number of soundtrack, pop and rock numbers.  
  
We pride ourselves on our fresh sound and on performing interesting arrangements of both traditional and contemporary pieces.  
  
Highlights include performing for two Nobel Peace Prize Laureates - former American vice-president, Al Gore and Archbishop Desmond Tutu.
